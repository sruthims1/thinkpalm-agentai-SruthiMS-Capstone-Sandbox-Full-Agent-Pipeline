Feature: Voyage Planning

The mock maritime app at http://localhost:5000/voyage supports end-to-end voyage planning for vessels.

Page: /voyage
The page shows a voyage register table with columns: Voyage ID, Vessel, Route, Departure, ETA, Distance, Bunker, ECA Zones, Status, Action.
Active voyages in piracy-risk areas show a red .departure-block banner with text "PIRACY ALERT: <vessel> — Active voyage <id> passes through high-risk area."

Pre-seeded voyages:
- VOY-2026-045: MV Oceanic Pioneer, Houston → Rotterdam, departure 2026-06-01, 5420 nm, VLSFO, 1850 MT bunker, 2 ECA zones (North Sea ECA, English Channel ECA), status: Planned, no piracy alert
- VOY-2026-046: MV Star Nour, Singapore → Chiba, departure 2026-06-17, 3320 nm, VLSFO, 980 MT bunker, no ECA zones, status: Active, piracy_alert: true (Malacca Strait / South China Sea), weather_routing: weather_deviation

UI elements on the page:
- Voyage register table: <table class="table table-hover mb-0"> inside .card-body
- Piracy alert banner: .departure-block (shown on page load without user interaction)
- ECA zone badge per row: <span class="badge bg-warning text-dark"> showing zone count
- No-ECA badge: <span class="badge bg-success"> with text "None"
- Status badge: Active = <span class="badge bg-success">Active</span>, Planned = <span class="badge bg-primary">Planned</span>
- Details button per row: <button onclick="showVoyageDetails('<id>')"> — shows #voyageDetailCard div
- Plan New Voyage button: [data-bs-target='#newVoyageModal']

Voyage detail panel (#voyageDetailCard, hidden by default):
- Route waypoints list
- ECA zone compliance alert
- Fuel planning table (fuel type, consumption, margin)
- Weather deviation alert: #weather-deviation-alert (text: "Typhoon in South China Sea. Route deviation recommended. ETA revised +12 hours.")
- Confirm Route Deviation button: #confirmDeviationBtn (submits POST /voyage/confirm-deviation)
- After confirming: flash message "Route deviation confirmed. ETA updated." and redirect to /voyage

Plan New Voyage modal (#newVoyageModal):
Form fields:
- select[name='vessel'] — options: "MV Oceanic Pioneer", "MV Star Nour"
- select[name='fuel_type'] — options: "VLSFO (0.5% Sulphur)", "MGO (ECA compliant)", "LNG"
- input[name='departure_port'] — text, required (e.g. "Port of Singapore")
- input[name='arrival_port'] — text, required (e.g. "Port of Rotterdam")
- input[name='departure_date'] — date, required
- input[name='speed_kts'] — number, required (valid range: 5–25 knots)
- input[name='bunker_qty'] — number, required (must be positive)
- input[name='distance_nm'] — number, required
- Submit: #createVoyageBtn → POST /voyage/new → redirect back to /voyage with flash message "Voyage VOY-2026-0XX created successfully."

Regulations: MARPOL Annex VI Regulation 14 (ECA zone sulphur 0.1% limit), IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3 (piracy BMP5), SOLAS Chapter V (weather routing), COLREGS 1972.
Safety classification: HIGH — incorrect fuel planning or ignored piracy alerts can endanger crew and vessel.
