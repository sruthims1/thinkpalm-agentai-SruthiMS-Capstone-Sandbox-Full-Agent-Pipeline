Feature: Crew Certification Expiry Management

The MarineOps system tracks STCW and flag-state certificates for all crew members aboard managed vessels.

Core Requirements:
- The system maintains a certification register with crew name, rank, certificate type, certificate number, issue date, and expiry date.
- Certificate status is automatically calculated daily: Valid (more than 30 days to expiry), Expiring Soon (within 30 days), Expired (past expiry date).
- A warning alert is displayed for any crew member whose certificate expires within 30 days.
- A critical alert is triggered and a notification is sent to the fleet manager and superintendent when a certificate expires within 7 days.
- Any expired certificate blocks vessel departure. A VESSEL DEPARTURE BLOCKED banner is displayed.
- The departure block cannot be overridden without superintendent authorisation.
- Authorised users can renew a certificate by entering a new expiry date and certificate number.
- Upon successful renewal, the system recalculates status and removes the departure block if all certificates are valid.
- A filter allows users to view crew by status: All, Expired, Expiring Soon, Valid.
- The system must display the number of days remaining or days overdue for each certificate.
- Minimum one valid Medical Fitness Certificate must be present on the vessel at all times.
- GMDSS certificate must be held by at least one navigating officer per watch.

Regulations: STCW Convention (as amended 2010), MLC 2006, Flag State Requirements.
Safety classification: CRITICAL — expired certificates can result in Port State Control detention and vessel unseaworthiness.
